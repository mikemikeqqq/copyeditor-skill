# Target-Journal Profile

Use only when the user specifies a target journal or publisher.

Record:

- journal and article type;
- verified official guidance date;
- word limits and abstract structure;
- heading and manuscript organization;
- spelling and citation style;
- table, figure, appendix, and supplementary-material rules;
- anonymization and title-page requirements;
- ethics, consent, data availability, funding, conflict, author contribution, and AI-use statements;
- reporting checklists or disciplinary standards.

Do not convert a copyedit into speculative journal imitation. Journal conventions modify presentation and compliance; they do not justify changing theory, results, or empirical claims.

If no target journal is supplied, use a journal-neutral profile and state that no journal-specific compliance review was performed.
